Generative models

PyTorch, Flask, React

TerrainGen

Generates playable 2D platformer levels in the style of SuperTux. A difficulty-conditioned convolutional VAE trained on 1,509 level segments proposes a level, then a physics-aware search pass adds the fewest platforms needed to make it possible to finish. That repair step raised medium-difficulty playability from 0.5% to 78.5% while keeping the tile mix the model learned.
