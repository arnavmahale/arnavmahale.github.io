Projects

Cup Pong Robot Arm

A Kinova Gen3 Lite arm that picks up a ping pong ball, winds up, and throws it into a rack of cups. Motion planning runs in ROS 2 and MoveIt 2 with inverse kinematics, and the gripper releases when the swinging joint crosses a set angle. It lands 75% of its shots across more than 50 physical trials.

Featured by Duke MEMS

Fresh or Rotten

Classifies produce as fresh or rotten from a photo. A fine-tuned EfficientNet-B0 covering 13 kinds of produce reaches 97.2% test accuracy, against 94.8% for a random forest on hand-built features and 66.4% for always guessing the majority class.

Try it. The model runs in your browser, and no image leaves your device.

Pick a photo to classify it.

TerrainGen

Generates playable 2D platformer levels in the style of SuperTux. A difficulty-conditioned convolutional VAE trained on 1,509 level segments proposes a level, then a physics-aware search pass adds the fewest platforms needed to make it possible to finish. That repair step raised medium-difficulty playability from 0.5% to 78.5% while keeping the tile mix the model learned.

Web app

Remote-Controlled Rover

A remote-controlled rover built from scratch on an aluminum extrusion frame, with a motor at each wheel and a dedicated motor driver for each one.

SAFE-T

An open-source audit of whether the data and AI tools behind street-safety spending see risk equally in every neighborhood. The pilot covers 65 census tracts in Durham, North Carolina, combining Census demographics, state crash records, and OpenStreetMap. Built with a four-person Duke team: it took second place at Duke's Society-Centered AI Hackathon, and we were invited to present it at the SCAI 2026 conference.

ClickLens

Predicts which of several candidate YouTube thumbnails will draw the most clicks. A fine-tuned EfficientNet-B0 scores each one as low, medium, or high click-through, ranks them, and uses Grad-CAM to show which parts of the image drove the score.

Try it. Compare thumbnails in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

The model sees the center square of each thumbnail, so that is what the results show.

Escape Room Puzzle Box

A four-stage physical puzzle on a Raspberry Pi. Scan the right RFID tag, say the keyword to an offline speech recognizer, show the camera a face and a red object, then enter the code those stages reveal on a keypad to release a servo lock. An LCD walks players through each step.

Smart Room Assistant

A Raspberry Pi you can talk to about the room you're in. It wakes on motion, transcribes your request on the device with Whisper, photographs the window, reads temperature and humidity, and asks Claude's vision model what to do. Then it answers out loud and moves a servo, a fan, and two LEDs to match.

Guardians of the Code

Checks pull requests against a company's written engineering policies, the kind a linter can't express. It turns policy documents into concrete rules, retrieves the relevant excerpts for each change, and has a language model flag violations with line numbers, citations, and suggested fixes. It runs automatically whenever a pull request is opened.
